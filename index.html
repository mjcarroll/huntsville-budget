What's in "Special Appropriations"? (FY2026 planned, not actual)

The FY2021–2025 actuals above only report "Special Appropriations" as a single lump per functional group. No historical budget book is archived, but the current FY2026 Adopted Budget itemizes every nonprofit/agency award — General Fund awards by category, Library funding, and the Lodging & Liquor Tax Fund's support for local museums and cultural institutions.

Source: City of Huntsville, Alabama, Annual Comprehensive Financial Reports (ACFR), FY2021–FY2025, available from the City's Finance & Budget page. Excludes proprietary/enterprise funds (e.g. Water Pollution Control, Parking & Public Transit) and fiduciary funds. The Budget Flow view's "Bond Proceeds & Transfers (net)" / "Added to Fund Balance" nodes stand in for each fund's "Other Financing Sources (Uses)" (debt issuance, bond premiums, transfers between funds), netted per fund so revenue in equals expenditure out. The multi-year trend views (Revenue & Expenditures, Fund Balance, Debt & Capital, Growth) use the ACFR's own 10-year statistical schedules, which reflect routine prior-period reclassifications and can differ slightly from the year-by-year figures in the Budget Flow view.
